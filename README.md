
How Marcoeconmic Condition Influence The Price Of Bitcoin📊
📌Overview 

This project analyze how key macroeconomic indicators such as interest rate, inflation data and unemployment rate data influence the price of bitcoin. The goal is to understand how liquidity conditions impact risk assets.

🎯Objective
To identify macroeconmic environments that favor bullish and bearsih trends in Bitcoin.

🛠️Tools Used
SQL(BigQuery) and PowerBI

📊Key Insights 
* Bitcoins tends to perform during periods when interest rates are low or declining, because declining or low interests rates signal improved liquidity in the markets as money because cheap to borrow.
* Bitcoin underperforms during High inflation periods because the Federal may tighten or raise interest rates to help control inflation, the tightening of interest rates may cause liquidity to dry up as money because expensive to borrow, which causes market participants to sell off risk assets and hold safe haven assets like Us treasuries.

  💡Conclusion
  Macroeconomic conditions signficantly influence Bitcoin price behavior, with liquidity cycles acting as a major driver of market direction.
